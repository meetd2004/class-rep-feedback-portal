Frequently Asked Questions

Portal Guidelines

Who can view the feedback I submit?

Only you (as the submission author) and the assigned MSc Cybersecurity Group B class representatives can view your submission and its replies. Other students cannot view or access it.

When can I edit or delete a submission?

You can update or permanently delete any of your submissions as long as its status is still New. Once a class representative posts a reply or moves it to In Review or Resolved, editing and deletion are locked.

How will I know when my class rep responds?

Open My Feedback after signing in to see updated status badges, response counts, and timestamped replies directly inside each feedback thread.
